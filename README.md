# THE COMPLETELY UNNECESSARY MEHUL CINEMATIC UNIVERSE (MCU)

> *"Preserving the legendary bouncing Mehul Manoharan centerpiece while escalating bureaucratic absurdity to unprecedented heights."*

A playful, zero-dependency, 100% fictional parody fan experience. Built around the classic bouncing "Mehul Manoharan" screensaver, upgraded with a 12-feature MCU ecosystem, interactive arcade mini-games, dynamic Monday mode gating, and accessible controls.

---

## 🌟 What's Inside

### 🌀 The Centerpiece: Mehul Bounce Arena
- **True-to-Original Bouncing Physics**: Re-engineered with `requestAnimationFrame` for buttery-smooth 60fps movement and zero memory leaks.
- **Scale Selector**: Tiny (24px), Standard (48px), or Enormous (70px).
- **Physics Styles**: Classic Text, Rubber Duck Mode 🦆 (with duck styling & badges), and Reverse Gravity 🌌.
- **Chaos Slider**: Smoothly dial velocity and jitter from `1` (*Mildly Normal*) to `10` (*Legally Questionable in a Cartoon Universe*).
- **Live Wall Bonk Tracker**: Real-time counter tallying every boundary collision.
- **7-Click Easter Egg**: Click the bouncing text 7 times quickly to unleash the secret rainbow bonanza!
- **Interactive Dancing Avatar**: Hover or tap Baby Mehul to make him dance (`baby mehul.jpg`), trigger speech bubbles, and burst harmless confetti.

## ⚡ Monday-Only Feature Gate & Demo Mode

The MCU dynamically detects whether today is Monday according to the user's local clock (`getDay() === 1`):
- **On Mondays**: The full *"Mehul Mondays"* chaos mode unlocks automatically with an active hazard ribbon banner.
- **On Tuesday–Sunday**: Features display a playful locked state (*"The Mehul Department is closed until Monday"*), while keeping the central Bouncing Arena and mini-game previews accessible.
- **Local Demo Preview Toggle**: A prominent button labeled **`[⚡ Preview Monday Mode]`** is present in the top navigation bar and in the banner. Users and evaluators can preview Monday chaos mode at any time with one click, without altering system time or using hidden developer backdoors.

## 🦺 Safety & Satirical Boundaries

- **100% Satire & Harmless Fiction**: All references to *"borderline illegal"*, *"charges"*, *"court"*, *"dance permits"*, *"audits"*, or *"biscuit redistribution"* are absurd cartoon parodies.
- **No Real-World Harm**: Contains zero real financial advice, no deceptive news, no actionable illegal guidance, no vandalism, no dangerous stunts, and no harassment or doxxing.
- **Privacy & Local Integrity**: Operates entirely client-side. Zero analytics, zero cookies, zero external trackers. High scores and unlocked achievements reside exclusively in your browser's `localStorage`.

## ♿ Accessibility & Motion Sensitivity

- **Reduce Chaos Mode**: Visible `[🦺 Reduce Chaos]` button in the top bar stops intensive animations, eliminates screen jitters, and slows movement to a gentle drift.
- **Automatic Motion Detection**: Fully respects system-level `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- **Audio Politeness**: **Zero autoplay audio**. Audio is muted by default and requires explicit user activation. Games feature dedicated silent visual modes.
- **Screen Reader Support**: Includes a skip navigation link (`Skip to main content`), semantic HTML landmarks, and an `aria-live="polite"` region announcing game events, unlocks, and modal changes.
- **Keyboard Navigation**:
  - `[Esc]`: Closes any open modal or mini-game.
  - `[A]` / `[R]`: Approve or Reject documents in the Bureaucracy mini-game.
  - `[Spacebar]`: Snooze attack in Boss Fight: Monday Itself.
  - `[Arrow Keys]` / `[A/D]`: Steer in Dodge Tax and hit beats in Unauthorized Dance Permit.
  - Full `Tab` focus order with distinct `:focus-visible` high-contrast outlines.
